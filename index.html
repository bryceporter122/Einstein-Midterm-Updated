About Me

My name is Albert Einstein. I was a theoretical physicist best known for developing the theory of relativity. I was born on March 14, 1879, in Ulm, Germany.

Throughout my career, I made major contributions to modern physics. My work on the photoelectric effect helped establish quantum theory, and my theory of relativity changed how scientists understand space and time.

In 1921, I received the Nobel Prize in Physics for my explanation of the photoelectric effect.
